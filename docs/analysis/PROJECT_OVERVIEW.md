# Project Overview

**Application Name:** Inventory Management System (IMS)
**Developer:** Techlink360
**Core Purpose:** A centralized, reactive system for managing retail inventory, tracking sales via a POS interface, managing procurement, and monitoring financial health.
**Target Users:** Small to medium retail business owners, warehouse managers, and sales personnel.
**Major Modules:**
- **Inventory Module:** Product tracking, category management, barcode support, and reorder alerts.
- **Sales Module (POS):** Real-time cart management, customer selection, and transaction processing.
- **Procurement Module:** Supplier management and purchase order tracking.
- **Finance & Analytics:** Expense tracking, profit/loss reporting, and stock level analysis.
- **Administration:** User management (RBAC), audit logging, and custom licensing.
